No account is required to use this app, and none is requested.

Launching the app for the first time takes the user straight into onboarding
and then into the app. We do not ask for a name, email, password, or any other
personal information at any point, and no registration screen is shown. The
onboarding questions (name and age) are optional and can be skipped.

The subscription can be purchased, used and restored entirely without an
account. Entitlements are tied to the App Store account, so Restore Purchase
works on any of the user's devices without registering with us.

Registering is offered but never required. Profile → Account → "Create an
account" explains that it backs up progress and makes the subscription
available on the user's other supported devices. Anything created beforehand
carries over, so choosing to register later — or never — costs the user
nothing.

The sign-in details below are only needed to review the Community tab, which is
account-based because posts, circles and the leaderboard are shared with other
members. Every other feature — streak tracking, daily check-ins, progress and
milestones, the panic/SOS tool, money-saved tracking, and the private video
vault — works without an account.

Steps to reproduce on a clean install:

1. Launch the app. It opens directly into onboarding; no sign-up is shown.
2. Skip the optional name and age questions.
3. Complete a purchase on the subscription screen.
4. The app opens to the dashboard. No registration prompt appears at any point.
