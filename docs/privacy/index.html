Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 12, 2026  ·  Last updated: June 12, 2026

Who we are

Stopper is a personal recovery companion app built and operated by Hussain Jaeh (hussaijaeh320@gmail.com). "We", "us", and "our" refer to the Stopper app and its operator.

We built Stopper to help people track their recovery journey in a private, judgment-free environment. Your data exists to make the app work for you — nothing else.

What data we collect

We only collect what the app needs to function:

  • Account information — your name or display name and email address, provided when you sign up.
  • Recovery profile — the habit you are quitting, your quit date, and the reason you shared during onboarding.
  • Check-ins — daily mood and craving-level entries you record voluntarily.
  • Relapse events — if you log a setback, we store the trigger and any note you add.
  • Community activity — posts, cheers, and circles you interact with. Posts are always attributed to your pseudonymous handle, never your real name.
  • App settings — your preferences such as daily reminders and anonymous mode.

We do not collect location data, contacts, camera or microphone access, advertising identifiers, or any data from other apps on your device.

How we use your data

  • To calculate and display your streak, progress charts, and milestones.
  • To show your community feed and leaderboard (using your pseudonymous handle only).
  • To send daily reminder notifications if you have enabled them.
  • To let you recover your account if you sign out or switch devices.

We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it to build profiles for third parties.

How your data is stored

Stopper uses Convex as its backend database and authentication provider. Your data is stored on Convex's infrastructure, which is hosted in the United States. Convex is SOC 2 Type II certified and encrypts data at rest and in transit.

We do not operate our own servers. Convex's privacy policy governs how they handle data on our behalf.

Community & anonymity

All community features use a pseudonymous handle (e.g. "SwiftEagle_42") derived automatically from your account — your real name is never displayed publicly. If you enable Stay anonymous in settings, your activity is attributed only to your handle and your display name is hidden from all community views.

Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account from within the app, all your personal data — including your recovery profile, check-ins, relapses, and community posts — is permanently deleted within 30 days.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correction — update inaccurate information in-app or by contacting us.
  • Deletion — delete your account and all associated data from within the app.
  • Portability — request an export of your data in a machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hussaijaeh320@gmail.com.

Children's privacy

Stopper is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EU/UK). We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you in-app. Continued use of Stopper after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
