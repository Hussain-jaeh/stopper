Hello,

Thank you for the additional review. We have removed the account requirement entirely — registration is now optional throughout the app.

**What changed in this build**

1. **Registration is optional at launch.** The first screen now presents "Continue without an account" as a full-width option above the sign-up choices, not as a secondary link. Choosing it takes the user straight into the app. No name, email, password, or any other personal information is requested or stored.

2. **In-App Purchases no longer require an account.** A user who continues without an account can view the subscription options, complete the purchase, and immediately access everything they bought. They are not asked to register before, during, or after the purchase. "Restore Purchase" is available on the same screen, and entitlements are tied to the App Store account rather than to any registration of ours.

3. **The onboarding questions are optional.** Name and age are now labelled optional and the user can continue without answering either.

4. **Only account-specific features require an account.** The Community tab — shared posts, circles, and the leaderboard, all of which are visible to other members — explains that it needs an account and offers to create one. Every other feature works without one: streak tracking, daily check-ins, progress and milestones, the panic/SOS tool, money-saved tracking, and the private video vault.

5. **Registering later is offered, never forced.** Profile → Account shows "Create an account", explained as a way to back up progress and make the subscription available on the user's other supported devices. Anything created before registering is carried over to the new account, so nothing is lost by choosing to register later — or not at all.

**Steps to reproduce on a clean install**

1. Launch the app.
2. On the first screen, tap **"Continue without an account"**.
3. Skip or answer the optional onboarding questions.
4. On the subscription screen, complete a purchase.
5. The app opens directly to the dashboard. No registration prompt appears at any point.

Please let us know if anything further is needed.
