## Who we are

Stopper is a personal recovery companion app built and operated by Hussain Jaeh (hussaijaeh320@gmail.com). "We", "us", and "our" refer to the Stopper app and its operator.

We built Stopper to help people track their recovery journey in a private, judgment-free environment. Your data exists to make the app work for you — nothing else.

---

## What data we collect

We only collect what the app needs to function.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Account information" icon="user">
    Your name or display name and email address, provided when you sign up.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Recovery profile" icon="heart">
    The habit you are quitting, your quit date, and the reason you shared during onboarding.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Check-ins" icon="calendar">
    Daily mood and craving-level entries you record voluntarily.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Community activity" icon="users">

  </Card>

We do **not** collect location data, contacts, camera or microphone access, advertising identifiers, or any data from other apps on your device.

---

## How we use your data

- To calculate and display your streak, progress charts, and milestones.
- To show your community feed and leaderboard using your pseudonymous handle only.
- To send daily reminder notifications if you have enabled them.
- To let you recover your account if you sign out or switch devices.

We do **not** sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it to build profiles for third parties.

---

## How your data is stored

Stopper uses [Convex](https://convex.dev) as its backend database and authentication provider. Your data is stored on Convex's infrastructure, hosted in the United States. Convex is SOC 2 Type II certified and encrypts data at rest and in transit.

We do not operate our own servers. Convex's [privacy policy](https://www.convex.dev/privacy) governs how they handle data on our behalf.

---

## Community & anonymity

All community features use a pseudonymous handle (e.g. "SwiftEagle_42") derived automatically from your account — your real name is never displayed publicly. If you enable **Stay anonymous** in settings, your display name is hidden from all community views.

---

## Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account from within the app, all your personal data — including your recovery profile, check-ins, relapses, and community posts — is permanently deleted within 30 days.
